# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Web; desktop and mobile browsers, not a native app.

## Users

One realtor and one assistant at Meadow Lane Realty & Development, with equal access to shared records. The assistant keeps work organized and assigns tasks to the realtor. Both use one dashboard in the initial MVP.

## Product Purpose

Replace scattered client and engagement tracking with a working shared CRM. [docs/SPEC.md](docs/SPEC.md) is the sole product brief; [docs/CONTEXT.md](docs/CONTEXT.md) owns domain vocabulary. Do not duplicate requirements here.

## Positioning

A small-business workspace: one shared pipeline, fixed stages, reversible archiving, no scoring or workflow builder. Every surface projects the same records.

## Operating Context

The realtor works away from a desk; shipped workflows must work on phones. The business also refers estate sales to a partner and takes personal construction jobs.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Use the MVP boundaries in [docs/SPEC.md](docs/SPEC.md). OpenSpec changes define proposed implementation and acceptance criteria. Calendar, Phone, automated reminders, and reporting are not inferred from examples.

## Brand Commitments

The business name is established. Quiet Cream (`static/dashboards/04-quiet-cream.html`) is the visual starting point for the shell, not an approved feature set or final design for every screen. No other brand assets are committed.

## Evidence on Hand

Static dashboards and screenshots are visual references only; fixture content is not real client data or evidence of expected functionality. [docs/checklists.md](docs/checklists.md) contains the 16 editable seed templates, not mandatory procedures. [docs/research/phone-module.md](docs/research/phone-module.md) contains unanswered discovery questions.

## Product Principles

Shared records, not copies. Real dates, not hand-maintained urgency. Checklists, not more pipeline columns. Archive, not irreversible deletion. Two equal users, with phone-usable workflows. No feature commitments from design references.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use legible text, reachable touch actions, visible focus, keyboard-operable controls, and clear feedback for two non-specialist users. Static comparison prototypes are exempt from production mobile requirements; the shipped app is not.

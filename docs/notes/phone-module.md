# Phone discovery — deferred

No provider, number, feature set, or delivery commitment exists. This is a question list, not requirements; see [requirements.md](../requirements.md).

- Value: which calls matter today, how many, who answers, and which problems justify a provider dependency and monthly cost?
- Scope: logging, click-to-call, caller ID, routing, voicemail, recording, transcription, or missed-call follow-up—which are actually wanted?
- Numbers: keep the realtor's personal cell, add a hosted number, or port an existing number? Confirm ownership, portability, pricing, setup, outage behavior, and spam reputation.
- Privacy: jurisdiction, consent, disclosure, retention, and who may access recordings/transcripts, before designing either.
- Records: activity versus a separate call record, multiple clients per call, unknown callers, and whether automated suggestions need human review. Never assume automatic pipeline movement.
- Workflow: where calling and notes belong, what the assistant handles, and what the business loses during an outage.

Confirm the business's needs and any number that must be retained before researching providers. Specify a separate change only if the business chooses to proceed.

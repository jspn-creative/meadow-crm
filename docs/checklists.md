# Default checklists

The 16 default checklist templates that ship with the app: one for every combination of opportunity type and stage. They are the seed content for the checklist settings of the **Pipeline** module, edited from within that module's own section of settings. Settings are organized into a **General** section and one section per module, so these live under Pipeline rather than in a settings area of their own.

They are a starting point, not a procedure. Every item is optional, every list is meant to be cut down to what the business actually does, and no stage change depends on an item being complete.

## How templates work

- A template is copied onto an opportunity when the user explicitly applies it from the opportunity's checklist. Moving an opportunity to a new stage never applies, clears, or duplicates a template.
- Applying copies. Later edits to a template in settings do not touch checklists already in progress, and an opportunity that has been through two stages keeps both sets of items.
- Items can be completed, given a due date, renamed, and removed on the opportunity. A dated item that passes its date is what the card shows as overdue; nothing about that is manual.
- An opportunity with no checklist is normal and fine. The board works without one.
- Templates are reset to the defaults in settings, which replaces the template content only — never the checklists on live opportunities.

## Buyer

**New lead** — someone who has just contacted the business.

- Log the initial contact attempt
- Send a follow-up message
- Record how the lead found the business
- Capture budget, area, and timeline
- Book the consultation

**Active lead** — qualifying, preparing, and searching.

- Hold the buyer consultation
- Confirm financing status or proof of funds
- Document must-haves, areas, and dealbreakers
- Execute the buyer representation agreement
- Configure the property search
- Schedule first showings
- Record showing feedback

**Under contract** — due diligence and closing.

- Distribute the executed contract to lender and title/escrow
- Verify the earnest money deposit
- Complete the inspection before the contingency deadline
- Finalize the repair addendum or credit
- Confirm the appraisal
- Confirm clear to close
- Review the title commitment and clear liens
- Sign the closing disclosure
- Complete the final walkthrough
- Sign, fund, and confirm recording

**Closed** — after the transaction.

- Recover keys, lockbox, and staging inventory
- Submit the brokerage compliance file
- Confirm commission disbursement
- Deliver the settlement gift
- Request a client review
- Add to the sphere-of-influence nurture list
- Schedule the home anniversary and annual check-in

## Seller

**New lead** — a homeowner considering a sale.

- Log the initial contact attempt
- Send a follow-up message
- Record how the lead found the business
- Ask about timing, reason for selling, and expected price
- Book the listing presentation

**Active lead** — preparing the home and marketing it.

- Hold the listing presentation
- Deliver the comparative market analysis
- Review the net sheet and pricing strategy
- Execute the listing agreement
- Prepare the required disclosure packages
- Arrange photography, floorplan, and video
- Finalize staging and repair punch list
- Publish the listing and syndication copy
- Track showing feedback
- Review incoming offers and issue counters

**Under contract** — due diligence and closing.

- Distribute the executed contract
- Verify the earnest money deposit
- Confirm the buyer's inspection and review the report
- Negotiate the repair addendum or credit
- Confirm the appraisal and value
- Review title and clear liens
- Deliver and obtain approval of required disclosures
- Complete the final walkthrough
- Sign, fund, and confirm recording

**Closed** — after the transaction.

- Recover lockbox, yard signs, and staging inventory
- Remove the listing and stop syndication
- Submit the brokerage compliance file
- Confirm commission disbursement
- Deliver the settlement gift
- Request a client review
- Add to the sphere-of-influence nurture list
- Schedule the home anniversary check-in

## Estate Sale

The business does not run estate sales. It works with a local company that coordinates, manages, and runs people's estate sales, so these templates cover the referral relationship and the handoff — not the sale itself. Most of these opportunities will sit in one stage for their whole life; that is expected.

**New lead** — someone has asked about an estate sale, or the partner company has sent the business something.

- Log the initial contact attempt
- Send a follow-up message
- Record how the family found the business
- Confirm the family wants an estate sale rather than a listing
- Capture the property location and the approximate timing
- Ask whether the family is already working with the partner company

**Active lead** — making the referral happen.

- Confirm the partner company is accepting the referral
- Make the introduction between the family and the partner company
- Share the property details and the family's timeline
- Log the partner company's contact and next step
- Note whether the family also needs a real estate service from the business

**Under contract** — the estate sale is booked and the family has committed.

- Confirm the booked date and scope with the partner company
- Confirm the referral arrangement and who gets what
- Track anything the business itself owes the family, if anything
- Note any follow-up the family has asked for

**Closed** — the estate sale is done.

- Confirm the sale took place
- Log any feedback the family shared
- Add the family to the nurture list if they may need real estate later
- Note whether the partner company referred anyone back
- Archive the opportunity

## Construction

Work the owner takes on personally for a client, as a side line rather than through a brokerage. These opportunities live on the same board as everything else.

**New lead** — someone has asked about a job.

- Log the initial contact attempt
- Send a follow-up message
- Record how they found the business
- Capture what the job is and roughly when they want it done
- Ask for a budget range

**Active lead** — scoping, pricing, and getting the job booked.

- Visit or review the site and confirm the scope
- Write the estimate or proposal
- Review the estimate with the client and get it approved
- Confirm the deposit or first payment
- Pull any permits needed for the work
- Source materials and confirm lead times
- Schedule the work and any subcontractors

**Under contract** — the work is underway.

- Track the schedule against the promised dates
- Track change orders and get them approved in writing
- Coordinate subcontractors and deliveries
- Flag anything that threatens the schedule
- Complete the work

**Closed** — the job is done and paid for.

- Complete the final walkthrough with the client
- Confirm final payment received
- Collect and file any permits, warranties, and receipts
- Request a review or referral
- Add the client to the nurture list
- Note any follow-up work they might want next

## Editing notes

- Items are written as single short actions in the imperative. Keep them that way; a long sentence becomes a task nobody completes.
- Financing, appraisal, and disclosure items apply to some clients only. Leave them in — an unchecked item the business skips is cheaper than a missing item it needed.
- The Closed lists are the most likely to be cut down. Post-close work that nobody does should not sit on every closed deal.
- Adding a tag, type, or stage later means adding its templates here too, so the settings matrix is never missing a cell.
